My Goals Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: 23rd Nov, 2026

My Goals Tracker is made by DevGarage ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your goal data stays on your phone

Your goal, tasks, daily answers, reminder time and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts. This data is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app reads your current wallpaper only to keep a copy on your device, so it can put it back when your goal ends.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or a US state with its own privacy law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: for your daily reminder.
  • Full-screen alarm (optional): to show the daily check-in over your lock screen when the reminder goes off.
  • Set wallpaper: to put your progress dots on your lock and home screen.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only): to read your current wallpaper so it can be kept and put back later.
  • Internet: only to load ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
